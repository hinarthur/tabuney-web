This Privacy Policy explains how Tabuney ("We", "Us" or "Our") collects, uses, stores and shares information when You use Our website and related services (the "Service"). It also explains Your choices and how to contact Us about Your Personal Data.

This policy describes Our data practices; it does not replace any separate consent required by applicable law. Where We rely on Your consent, You can withdraw it as explained below.

Definitions

  • Account means an account used to access member features of the Service.
  • Tabuney (also referred to as "We", "Us" or "Our") refers to the individual operator described above, trading as Tabuney.
  • Personal Data means information relating to an identified or identifiable individual.
  • Usage Data means information about how the Service is accessed or used.
  • Cookies are small files stored by a website on Your device. Browser storage, such as local storage and session storage, can also hold information on Your device.
  • Service Providers are third parties that help deliver or operate the Service.
  • You means the person accessing or using the Service.

Information We Collect

The information We process depends on the features You use:

  • Account and profile information: Your email address, name, Google account identifier, profile picture URL, registration source, language preferences and account-related timestamps.
  • Membership and preferences: membership tier, subscription status, content unlocks, feed display and language settings, game collection or wish-list entries, and ratings or other interactions You save.
  • AI conversations: questions You submit, relevant conversation history, generated answers, cited sources, feedback and comments, conversation timestamps, the game or rulebook involved, and technical usage details such as model, question category and token counts. Conversations may be linked to Your Account and a stored display name.
  • Feedback and surveys: suggestions You send about a flowchart or one of its steps, and Your answers to short surveys, together with the game, page and language involved and the time sent. If You are signed in, these are linked to Your Account. If You are not signed in, they are stored without any identifier: no IP address, Cookie or session is recorded with them.
  • Messages and enquiries: messages You send through the Service, conversation participants and read status, and the name, email address, subject and content You provide when contacting Us.
  • Payments and subscriptions: Stripe customer, checkout, payment, invoice and subscription identifiers, transaction amounts and currencies, payment status, subscription periods and cancellation settings. We also retain checkout consent records, including the wording and time of your separate confirmations, the policy versions shown, the purchase and Stripe session reference, and the confirmation email address and sending status. Payment details entered in Stripe Checkout are handled by Stripe; Our transaction records contain payment references rather than full card details.
  • Usage and technical information: search terms or scanned barcodes, search results, pages or content viewed, language selections, timestamps, source (website or app), and diagnostic or error information. Requests to Our servers include an IP address and browser information, which may also be processed in infrastructure or security logs.

Some features require information to work: for example, Google account information is needed for Google sign-in, payment information is needed for a purchase, and a question is needed for an AI answer. If You do not provide the required information, the relevant feature may be unavailable.

Google Sign-In

When You choose to sign in with Google, We receive account information such as Your Google identifier, email address and verification status, name and profile picture URL. We use this information to authenticate You, create or update Your Account, and display Your profile. Google also processes information under its own privacy policy.

AI Conversations and External AI Services

When You use Our AI question-and-answer features, We process Your question together with relevant earlier messages and game or rulebook context to generate an answer. Depending on the feature, model routing and service availability, this content may be sent to Google (Gemini), OpenAI or Anthropic (Claude). If an initial provider fails, a fallback provider may receive the same request. Personal information You include in a question or earlier message may therefore be included in the content sent to these providers.

We store conversation content and related usage details to provide conversation history, continue discussions, manage usage limits, investigate errors, review feedback and evaluate answer quality and service performance. AI providers may process or retain submitted content under the applicable API terms, account settings and privacy policies. Their retention and model-training arrangements depend on those terms and settings; this policy does not promise zero retention or exclusion from training by every provider. Please avoid entering sensitive information or other people's Personal Data unless it is necessary and You are entitled to do so.

If You ask questions without signing in, the conversation is stored without any link to an Account. To enforce the daily question limit for visitors who are not signed in, We keep a one-way, salted hash of the requesting IP address together with a question count in Our server cache. It is not stored in Our database, is not linked to an Account, uses no cookie, and expires automatically at the end of the day (UTC).

Clearing Conversation History Is Not Data Deletion

The "clear conversation" feature hides the conversation from Your visible history and excludes it from subsequent conversation continuation. It does not erase the conversation or its messages from Our database. The stored content and associated usage records remain available for service administration, troubleshooting, quality review and usage statistics, subject to the retention criteria below. Clearing history also does not delete copies already processed by an external AI provider.

To request deletion of stored conversations or other Personal Data, please email info@tabuney.com and identify the Account and conversations concerned. We will assess the request under applicable law and explain any information that must be retained and why. See "Your Privacy Rights and Requests" below.

Cookies, Browser Storage and Usage Records

We do not use Google Analytics. We use Cookies and browser storage to support features such as authentication, request security and remembering Your choices. Removing Google Analytics does not remove the first-party usage records described in this policy.

  • Authentication and security: session and CSRF Cookies support sign-in and protect requests. Authentication tokens may also be stored in browser storage for access to member features.
  • Preferences and diagnostics: browser storage may remember Your theme or language choice and hold sign-in diagnostic information. Local storage can persist across visits; session storage generally lasts for the browser tab's session. Cookie lifetimes depend on the Cookie and its expiry settings.
  • Surveys: choosing "Not now" is remembered in session storage for the current tab only. To discourage automated abuse, a survey is loaded together with a signed, time-stamped token that is sent back with Your answers; it is not stored in a Cookie or linked to You. Sending feedback or a survey answer without signing in does not set any Cookie.
  • First-party usage records: We keep server-side search and content-view records, which may be linked to Your Account when You are signed in. Some measurements, such as daily page-view and language-selection counts, are stored as aggregate counts without a visitor identifier. These records are separate from Google Analytics.

You can block or delete Cookies and clear browser storage through Your browser settings. This may sign You out, reset preferences or prevent some features from working. External services You use, such as Google sign-in and Stripe Checkout, may also use Cookies or similar technologies under their own policies.

How We Use Your Information

  • To authenticate users, manage Accounts, remember preferences and provide member features.
  • To answer AI questions, maintain conversation history, handle feedback and manage usage limits.
  • To process purchases, subscriptions and content unlocks, and maintain transaction records.
  • To deliver messages, respond to enquiries and communicate about Your Account, purchases, service changes or security issues.
  • To understand searches and content use, diagnose faults and improve the Service.
  • To prevent abuse and fraud, protect the Service, comply with legal obligations and resolve disputes.

Where applicable data protection law requires a lawful basis, We rely on performance of Our agreement with You for requested account, AI and paid services; legal obligations for required records and disclosures; and legitimate interests in operating, securing and improving the Service, subject to Your rights and interests. Where consent is required, We obtain it separately and allow You to withdraw it.

Who Receives Your Information

We share information for the purposes described in this policy, including with:

  • Google: for Google sign-in and, when selected for an AI request, Gemini processing. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • OpenAI and Anthropic: for AI requests as described above. See OpenAI's Privacy Policy and Anthropic's Privacy Policy. Applicable API terms and account settings also govern processing.
  • Stripe: for checkout, payments and subscription management. We may provide Your email address and purchase or customer references and receive payment and subscription status information. See Stripe's Privacy Policy.
  • Infrastructure and support providers: providers of hosting, storage, network delivery and communications may process information needed to operate and support the Service.
  • Other message participants: messages and associated sender information are shared with the participants in the conversation You choose to use.
  • Authorities and professional advisers: where disclosure is required by law or necessary to handle legal claims, investigate abuse or protect people's rights and safety.
  • Business successors: if the Service is involved in a merger, acquisition or asset transfer, relevant information may be transferred as part of that transaction. We will notify You if Your information becomes subject to a different privacy policy.
  • Other recipients You authorise: where You request a disclosure or give any consent required for an additional purpose.

Retention of Your Information

Retention depends on the type of information and why it is needed. The criteria below apply rather than a single fixed retention period for all records:

  • Account and preference records: while needed to provide Your Account and associated features, and afterwards only where needed for legal obligations, security or disputes.
  • AI conversations and feedback: while needed for conversation history, service administration, troubleshooting, quality review and usage analysis. Clearing visible history does not start automatic deletion; You can make a separate deletion request.
  • Feedback and survey answers: while needed to review and act on the feedback and to analyse how the Service is received. Anonymous answers cannot be traced back to You, so they cannot be located for a deletion request.
  • Messages and enquiries: while needed to deliver the conversation, respond to the enquiry, handle follow-up issues or resolve disputes.
  • Payment and subscription records: for managing purchases and entitlements and meeting applicable accounting, tax, fraud-prevention and legal requirements. Checkout consent and confirmation records are retained as needed to evidence purchase instructions, handle cancellation requests and resolve disputes, subject to those legal requirements.
  • Usage and diagnostic records: while needed to assess service use, investigate faults or abuse and maintain security. Aggregate statistics that no longer identify individuals may be retained separately.

When assessing a deletion request, We consider these purposes and any applicable legal retention requirements. Deletion from active systems may not immediately remove copies in backups; any remaining copies are subject to the relevant backup retention arrangements. External providers may have separate retention obligations and processes.

Your Privacy Rights and Requests

Depending on the law that applies to You and the circumstances, Your rights may include:

  • Requesting access to, or a copy of, Your Personal Data.
  • Correcting inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Requesting deletion of Your Account, stored AI conversations or other Personal Data.
  • Objecting to processing or requesting that processing be restricted.
  • Receiving eligible information in a portable format.
  • Withdrawing consent for processing that relies on consent, without affecting the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal.
  • Making a complaint to the relevant data protection authority.

You can make a request by emailing info@tabuney.com with an optional subject "Privacy request". No special wording, form or email subject is required to exercise Your rights. Please describe what You want to access, correct or delete and, where possible, contact Us from the email address associated with Your Account. For conversation requests, include enough information to identify the relevant conversation, such as the game and approximate date. Please do not send passwords, authentication tokens or card details.

We may ask for proportionate information to verify Your identity and clarify the request before disclosing or changing data. Under UK data protection law, We normally respond without undue delay and within one month. Where the law permits extra time for a complex request or multiple requests, We will notify You within the initial month and explain why. Any lawful request for identity information or clarification, and its effect on the response period, will be explained. Requests are normally free; We will explain any fee or refusal permitted by law. These rights are not absolute: for example, some transaction information may need to be retained to comply with legal obligations. Requests about data held independently by Google, Stripe or an AI provider may also need to be directed to that provider.

Signing out, clearing browser storage, disconnecting Google access or clearing visible conversation history does not by itself delete information stored by Tabuney. Withdrawing access or requesting deletion may affect the features We can provide.

UK Privacy Complaints

If You have concerns about Our use of Your Personal Data, please contact Us so that We can investigate. You can also complain to the UK's Information Commissioner's Office (ICO): ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint. Contacting Us first does not remove Your right to complain to the regulator or seek a legal remedy.

International Processing

Tabuney and its Service Providers may process or store information in countries other than the country where You live, including when an external AI or payment service handles a request. Data protection laws may differ between countries. Where applicable law requires safeguards for an international transfer, We must use an appropriate transfer mechanism and safeguards; using the Service alone is not treated as consent to a transfer that requires separate consent. You can contact Us for information about the providers and transfer arrangements relevant to Your data.

Security of Your Personal Data

We use reasonable measures to protect Personal Data, but no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is completely secure. Please protect Your Account and avoid sharing information that is unnecessary for the feature You are using.

Children's Privacy

Our Service does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from anyone under the age of 13. If You are a parent or guardian and You are aware that Your child has provided Us with Personal Data, please contact Us. If We become aware that We have collected Personal Data from anyone under the age of 13 without verification of parental consent, We take steps to remove that information from Our servers.

If We need to rely on consent as a legal basis for processing Your information and Your country requires consent from a parent, We may require Your parent's consent before We collect and use that information.

Links to Other Websites

Our Service may contain links to other websites that are not operated by Us. If You click on a third party link, You will be directed to that third party's site. We strongly advise You to review the Privacy Policy of every site You visit.

We have no control over and assume no responsibility for the content, privacy policies or practices of any third party sites or services.

Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this policy as the Service or Our data practices change. We will publish the revised policy on this page and update the "Last updated" date above when the content changes. For material changes, We will provide a prominent notice or other notification where required by applicable law, and obtain any fresh consent required before the relevant processing begins.
